The Disconnect in Modern Event Planning.

Spatial Anxiety

2D floor plans fail to show couples if a large Poruwa or 20 banquet tables will actually fit in their physical venue.

Budget Blindness

Traditional planning relies on abstract spreadsheets, causing severe 'sticker shock' when aesthetic dreams collide with real-world LKR prices.

Fragmented Tools

Couples are forced to juggle venue visits, PDF brochures, and separate budgeting apps, leading to cognitive overload and logistical errors.

The ARvivaharata Solution

We combine state-of-the-art Augmented Reality with intelligent AI to create an immersive, seamless wedding planning experience.

Commercialization & Future Roadmap

From Academic Prototype to Industry Disruption.

This platform represents a highly deployable B2B and B2C commercial tool for the Sri Lankan event sector.

Phase 01

Remote Sales for Venues

Banquet halls can offer this app as a 'try-before-you-buy' tool, reducing the friction of venue sales.

Phase 02

Payment Gateway Integration

Future updates will link the AI Vendor Cart directly to local payment gateways (like PayHere) for instant booking confirmation.

Phase 03

Multi-User Collaboration

Integrating Apple's Multipeer Connectivity to allow wedding planners and couples to view and edit the same AR scene from different devices simultaneously.
